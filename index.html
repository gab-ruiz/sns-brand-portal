    Logo

    A refined, confident logotype that expresses SNS as one connected, end-to-end global supply chain partner. Simple, timeless, and built to extend across a broader brand system.

    Emblem breakdown

    The two “S” terminals align with the diagonal of the “N”, so the logotype reads as one connected movement.

    Clear space and minimum size

    Clear space equals twice the gap between the emblem and the logotype. Never reproduce the logo below the minimum sizes.

    Digital minimum

    Print minimum
